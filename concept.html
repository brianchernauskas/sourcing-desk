Sourcing Desk.
A Proxima subscription service

Your vendor remembers every concession. Does your team?

When a sourcing engagement closes, the savings are banked and the team moves on. The knowledge built up over those months — what your vendor conceded, where they pushed back, which of your own business units drove the overage — leaves with it. Sourcing Desk keeps that knowledge alive between deals, so your next renewal does not start from zero.

The problem

Renewal cycles are asymmetric, and the asymmetry is not in your favour

Your account team at a major vendor has your entire history in a CRM: every discount granted, every deadline you blinked at, every quarter-end you signed in. They will have that record in front of them at your next renewal.

You will have a contract, a handful of emails, and whoever is still in the seat. Eighteen to twenty-four months is long enough for the people who ran the last negotiation to move on, and for the reasoning behind every clause to become unrecoverable.

What it is

Three things, held continuously between engagements

01

A live record of your estate

Every contract, renewal date, and commitment tracked in one place, with the negotiation history attached — what was conceded, what was refused, and why. Maintained by the people who were in the room, not reconstructed from a document repository afterwards.

02

Market position, when you need it

Benchmarks and vendor playbooks calibrated to your specific footprint, plus alerts when a vendor's pricing or licensing model shifts in a way that affects you. Not a research library you have to go searching through.

03

Preparation before the conversation

A working session ahead of each renewal: where you stand, what the vendor will open with, what is realistically achievable, and where your leverage actually is this cycle. You walk in prepared rather than reactive.

How it works

Three levels of engagement

Every level is capped and defined. You know exactly what you are buying, and we know exactly what we have committed to — which is what keeps the service responsive rather than gradually overloaded.

Level 01

Intelligence

The record and the market view, maintained continuously. Self-serve.

  • Benchmarks calibrated to your footprint
  • Vendor playbooks and licensing-change alerts
  • Full access to Proxima's negotiation planning tools
Level 02 — most relevant for most clients

Desk

Everything in Intelligence, plus a named contact and a set allocation of advisory time each quarter.

  • Pre-negotiation preparation session per renewal
Level 03

Embedded

A Proxima sourcing lead working inside your team on a set number of days per month.

  • Works to your priorities, in your process
  • Suits teams carrying more renewals than headcount
  • Scoped and priced individually
Scope

What this deliberately does not do

Worth being direct about the boundaries, because a subscription that quietly becomes everything serves nobody well.

It does not run your negotiation

Sourcing Desk prepares you and reviews what you are about to sign. When a deal warrants Proxima at the table owning the outcome, that is a separate engagement — and we will tell you when we think it is worth it.

It is not unlimited advisory

Hours are defined per level. This is deliberate: an uncapped service degrades for everyone the moment one client has a difficult quarter.

It is not a research subscription

We are not trying to replace your analyst relationships. This is specific to your estate and your history, which is the part a research firm cannot hold.

It does not share your data

Your pricing and contract terms are yours. Anything that informs broader benchmarks is aggregated and anonymised, under terms agreed with you in writing first.

The ask

We are running this as a pilot first

Sourcing Desk is a new offering and we would rather build it with a small number of clients than launch it at everyone and get it wrong. We are looking for a handful of organisations to run it with us for six months, at no cost, in exchange for honest feedback on what is useful and what is not.

What you get

Level 02 — Desk, in full, for six months. Named contact, advisory time, renewal preparation, tooling access.

What it costs

Nothing. Pricing is being set with pilot participants, not before them. No commitment to continue afterwards.

What we ask

A short conversation each month about what is working, and permission to use anonymised findings to shape the service.

Who it suits

Organisations with a meaningful IT contract portfolio and more renewals in a year than the team can properly prepare for.
